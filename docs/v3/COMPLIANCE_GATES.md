# Release compliance gates — 14 November 2026
Public publishing, personalized recommendations, marketplace sales, DMs, performance leaderboards, advertising, payments and brokerage execution remain disabled.

A legal disclaimer, a rule match or a classifier result is not registration or compliance approval. The local screening layer gives snippets/reasons, permits private benign learning, routes risky Team sharing toward review, records appeals and keeps public publication closed. A trained classifier and a staffed review/appeal operation are not configured.

| Gate | Required evidence | Current state |
|---|---|---|
| RA / IA classification | Qualified Indian counsel determines currently applicable activity/registration and effective dates | BLOCKED |
| Public financial research | Counsel-approved scope, moderation, conflicts, audit, entitlements and owner authorization | BLOCKED |
| Privacy / minors / DPDP | Controller/processor terms, phased applicability, age assurance, retention, grievance, deletion/backup process | NOT APPROVED |
| Dataset rights | Concrete display/cache/history/derived/Team/public permissions and reviewer record | BLOCKED |
| Paid services | Explicit purchase/provisioning authority, pricing, GST/invoicing, consumer/refund and payment review | NOT AUTHORIZED |
| Consumer access | Real local Google and email-code journeys plus explicit later production/access authorization | BLOCKED |
