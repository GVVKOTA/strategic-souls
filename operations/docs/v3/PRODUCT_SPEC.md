# Strategic Souls V3 product specification
Owner: Global Vertex Vectors. Research value: turn a question into an accountable record, including its opposing case and predeclared invalidation.

## Three domains, five mobile destinations
Strategic Global contains Markets, the cost-labelled manual portfolio, India mechanisms, optional vendor calendar and source health. Social Community contains Body identity and private Team research. Soul Intelligence contains creation, Library, Scan Builder, evaluations, version comparison, educational options mathematics and Graveyard.

Mobile primary navigation is Home, Markets, Create (+), Teams, Profile. Home exposes Library, Graveyard and a secondary research toolkit. Desktop adds a collapsible sidebar. Beginner, Standard and Advanced are presentations in the same creation flow; they do not create separate products.

## First Soul
Idea: title, hypothesis, topic and horizon. Evidence: reasoning, opposing case, invalidation and dated reference cards. Save & track: explicit audience, review date, snapshot preview, private draft or committed version. The first input is near the top of the phone viewport. Incomplete drafts are accepted; committed hypotheses require meaningful countercase/invalidation and a future review date.

Saves use a stable request ID and a complete request fingerprint. Revisions use the expected current head, one transaction and an immutable parent reference. Reviews append to the exact evaluated version. An expired deadline means READY_TO_REVIEW, not a successful or failed trade. Supported/invalidated reviews are researcher assessments. A unique thesis has no standalone historical win rate.

## Bodies and Teams
Handles normalize NFKC, accept a conservative ASCII alphabet and are unique case-insensitively in PostgreSQL. Public profile opt-in never publishes private Souls or holdings. Personal research and Storage are private by default. The Body label is presentation configuration.

Owner/Admin manage membership; Editors author research; Reviewers discuss and review; Viewers read. Owner privilege cannot be removed or granted through the ordinary member editor. Invitations expire in 48 hours, are hashed at rest, optionally handle-bound and consumed once. Team rooms expose comments, tasks, shared research and attribution. They use explicit 15-second polling, not invented presence or typing indicators.

## Honest unavailable states
Numerical data is null until identity, source rights, timestamps and session are established. Scan definitions may be authored without an entitled dataset, but evaluation fails closed. Portfolio figures are acquisition cost by currency, without FX conversion or invented current valuations. Options outputs come only from explicitly hypothetical inputs.

## Targets and limits
Ten-second comprehension and a first Soul in roughly two minutes remain human usability targets; neither was achieved/claimed by screenshots or automation. In-memory drafts do not survive reload. Full conflict-free multi-user journeys, notifications, public publishing and complete account/Storage deletion need further integration and review.
