## Storage, jobs and operating limits

- A scheduled ingestion cycle runs every two minutes. It acquires a D1 lease per provider account and consumes its minute/day budgets. It attempts up to four bounded fetches per cycle across all grants. Responses are normalized by provider version, canonical instrument, native interval, observation and receipt time. D1 holds an immutable raw series per snapshot with a maximum of 3,000 bars and 800 KB per row. Expiry is the earlier of retention and grant expiry. Readers reject expired and superseded grants immediately, and ingestion prunes expired rows. A legitimate upstream authorization failure pauses that grant until it is revalidated. Transient failures back off, and throttling honors `Retry-After`.
- Eligible shared scans, charts, dashboard prices, heatmaps, Formula Lab and comparison use the same stored history. Completed intraday observations honor session boundaries and configured delay. Weekly/monthly series use complete eligible daily periods. Shared prices represent a completed close and never a streaming quote. EOD sector returns use compatible closes from the same session. Nifty industry heatmap group returns weight each available stock equally. The UI exposes timestamps, unavailable data, coverage, and required attribution. ECB reference rates retain official-source attribution.
- Jobs persist private account-scoped checkpoints and select the latest permitted snapshot received at or before submission per instrument. A minute scheduler rotates between jobs, leasing up to two 15-instrument shared batches or five-instrument owner batches per invocation. Formula evaluation has bounded per-instrument history and AST size. For fixed formula and warm-up, total work scales with the number of selected instruments. Memory scales with the bounded batch and bar series. Shared batches group D1 reads per grant. One job has at most one active batch lease. Reconciliation distinguishes matches, nonmatches, insufficient history, deferred work and failures. Cancellation and zero evaluated results release quota. The explicitly approved X7.8 allowance of 20 runs per IST day across modes remains in effect. Job checkpoints and stored scan proofs omit licensed raw price series and price/volume levels. Use Asset Research for the current permitted price history. Read-time checks protect derived results if the grant expires or changes.
- At the configured maximum, ingestion starts at most 120 upstream reads per hour across providers. A cold 500-symbol single-resolution seed needs at least 250 minutes if every read succeeds, and multiple 500-symbol intervals may exceed the time available to keep data current at that cadence. A 500-symbol shared scan with a ready store has a scheduler floor of 17 successful minute ticks, plus any queueing and finalization. Owner batches have a lower throughput cap to protect the private adapter. Per unique symbol/interval, steady-state storage includes every retained snapshot until expiry. Estimate it from measured payload size × daily refresh count × retention days across the proposed instrument mappings. Measure actual D1 size/query use, queue latency, Worker CPU, provider limits and coverage before any rollout. Cataloging an interval or seeing a ready slot does not establish 500-symbol coverage. [D1 platform limits](https://developers.cloudflare.com/d1/platform/limits/) · [Workers limits](https://developers.cloudflare.com/workers/platform/limits/) · [Cron Triggers](https://developers.cloudflare.com/workers/configuration/cron-triggers/)
- Owner Data Connections diagnostics expose mapped, fetched and due instrument intervals, check range, pauses and masked errors. Refresh telemetry reports rejected bar counts and duration. For each planned interval, verify actual completed market events at market open, after close, weekends and holidays. Check exchange calendars before expanding historic or future dates and observe real 500-member latency and coverage across the provider's permitted windows. Increase throughput or use a documented, contract-permitted bulk delivery if measured windows cannot meet the acceptance gate.

## Deployment, smoke tests and rollback

1. Run `node operations/scripts/check-x2-release.mjs .wrangler/x8-verified`, `node operations/scripts/run-framework.mjs build`, `node operations/scripts/scan-secrets.mjs`, and `uv run --project backend/quant pytest backend/quant/tests -q`. Verify the build points to Worker `strategic-souls`, D1 `ss`, the existing private VPC binding, and the two expected Cron triggers. Verify the approved snapshot, actual store coverage, quota and provider rights with a separate isolated preview before merging.
2. Record the verified production Worker version and D1 Time Travel bookmark. Confirm the physical schema and migration history on the intended remote D1 binding. If absent, apply `drizzle/0012_x78_market_scans.sql` followed by `drizzle/0013_x8_shared_history.sql` through the existing production migration process, once each. The 0013 column additions are non-idempotent. Reconcile the tracked migration state before retrying any interrupted operation. Verify both migrations on a clean local D1 and on a staging copy with the X7.8 schema.
3. After review and activation approval, use the existing GitHub-to-Cloudflare Workers Builds pipeline. Verify the running Git SHA, X8 revision marker, serving version, migration and Cron state. Import the approved constituent delivery and verify the entitlement gates and populated store before opening access. Preserve the owner adapter and monitor its existing health.
4. Register or use two independent, consenting ordinary accounts in signed-off staging and production. Verify owner access separately. In each scanner mode choose the default, run a known indicator or pattern, and record selected/evaluated/matched/unmatched/insufficient/deferred/failed, data event/receipt times, job duration, attribution and remaining quota. Repeat during an authorized market session and after close. Verify canceled work refunds the allowance and cross-account job access returns 404. Check chart parity, heatmap stock cells and equal weighting, cross-asset comparison, signup/signin, controlled recovery mail and mobile navigation. Ordinary account tests use no owner credentials. Record actual account outcomes and release timestamp without personally identifying account data.
5. On a grant incident, disable the grant in Data Connections to deny reads and stop refresh. If the release itself needs rollback, select the last verified Worker version in Cloudflare Deployments and recheck its release SHA, owner access and anonymous restricted behavior. Leave the additive schema available for recovery. Review queued X8 jobs and stale reservations before re-enabling the scheduler. Keep the new release out of production until required feed, constituent, verification and rollback gates are satisfied.
