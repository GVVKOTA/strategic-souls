## 2. Highest Lawfully Permitted Scope Architecture Implemented

To comply with Phase 0.5 and Phase 3.5 without confusing raw exchange redistribution with derived screening:

1. **Derived-Only Shared Screening (`rightsMode: "DERIVED_ONLY"`):**
   - Requires `verified: true`, `analysis: true`, `storage: true`, `derivedDisplay: true`, active `expiresAt`, active `constituentGrant`, and verified `creditWindow`, while keeping `display: false` and `redistribution: false`.
   - Permits server-side ingestion into the expiring D1 snapshot table (`x8_shared_history_snapshot`), multi-batch Nifty 500 Soul Scan evaluation, and returning **derived outcomes only** (`PASS`/`FAIL`/`UNKNOWN`, `matched`/`unmatched` counts, formula, coverage, universe version, attribution).
   - **Strictly blocks** raw price display, raw OHLCV candles, raw `metrics` (close/open/high/low/volume/high20/low20), `datasets`, Asset Research charts, Global 8-card raw quotes, sector quotes, price alerts, and CSV raw price exports.
2. **Full Display + Derived (`rightsMode: "DISPLAY_AND_DERIVED"`):**
   - Additionally requires `display: true` and `redistribution: true` backed by a verified exchange display/redistribution license.
   - Unlocks Asset Research charts, dashboard index cards (BharatStock indices only when mapped), sector quotes, and raw scan metrics.
3. **Production Activation Status (25 Nov 2026):**
   - Neither `INDIANAPI` (Free Plan, 500/mo, close-only) nor `BHARATSTOCK` (Free Tier, 60/day, caching & public end-user service forbidden by ToS) currently qualifies for production activation.
   - Therefore, production D1 `x2_providers` remains **unseeded (`0 rows`)** and public NSE/Nifty 500 scanning remains **honestly gated** until the owner completes the finite activation checklist in `PUBLIC_LAUNCH_REPORT.md`.
