# Strategic Souls X1

Release contract, 17 November 2026. Same project `appgprj_6ae0ae7364a481919dfb1af2f4fe17f0`; same restricted owner audience and existing URL.

## Product structure

Exactly three main dashboards, in order: **Global**, **Strategy**, **Community**. Account, search, data health and the existing research archive are utilities. The existing `/legacy`, asset and legal routes remain available. Native Sites sign-in is the active identity boundary; email is never an ownership key. Independent consumer authentication is not configured.

### Global

Overview presents actual official daily FX references and US yield observations when reachable, then the interactive exchange globe and private workspace summary. Watchlists support create, rename, reorder, add/remove instruments and recoverable archive. Manual portfolios use an append-only transaction ledger with server optimistic concurrency, moving-average acquisition cost, cash, fees, realized gains and separate dividends. No invented market valuation or currency aggregation. Market Explorer groups Markets, Sectors, Bonds, Capital, Flows, FX, Commodities, Crypto and Macro; unserved categories explain their exact data gap and link to primary sources.

The native WebGL globe loads as a separate component. It uses a shaded sphere, Natural Earth coastline vectors, 24 exchange identities, 21 city clusters, geographic arcs, drag, keyboard rotation, bounded zoom, a pause control and a List alternative. Motion pauses out of view, on hidden tabs, selection and user input, and respects reduced motion. City coordinates are not exact exchange premises. Only verified 2026 NYSE/Nasdaq scheduled cash sessions are evaluated; all unconnected calendars remain unknown. Live halts are not monitored. Index quotes are separately unavailable.

### Strategy

Exactly three menus: Soul Scan, Market Radar, Formula Lab. Soul Scan has exactly Simple and Advanced modes over the same versioned typed AST. Simple supports editable draggable conditions and accessible reorder buttons, ALL/ANY joins and conversion where representable. Advanced supports bounded mathematical/boolean expressions, safe function examples and positional errors. Current source-backed execution filters the eligible ECB daily FX universe. It saves rule, eligible observations, actual result explanations, timestamps and SHA-256 in an immutable scan snapshot. Missing inputs are UNKNOWN.

All runs belong to Scan History. Only explicit retirement, invalidation or reviewed failure enters the Graveyard, with append-only reviews and an explicit restore event. No probability, artificial accuracy, automated formula rewriting or collective learning is active. Market Radar opens editable daily FX filters; it shares one vendor display calendar and primary-source release links. Formula Lab calculates BSM/Greeks, implied volatility, CAGR and XIRR from user assumptions. Corporate-action-adjusted historical equity investment scenarios remain unavailable until their data exists.

### Community

Native signed-in accounts can create a unique case-normalized Body handle, editable bio and initials avatar; create private groups; invite exact Bodies by expiring hashed code; assign roles; contribute versioned shared Souls; resolve conflicting revisions; restore prior content as a new revision; publish exact revisions to the authorized group feed; set one Super per account/post; and add/edit/soft-remove threaded comments. Group owners/admins can review reports and moderate. Every direct-ID path checks server membership. Public posting is disabled. Invitations do not expand the Site audience. Photo upload, external consumer signup and meaningful notifications await their actual infrastructure.

## Deliberate limits and next increments

- No universal live market feed. Official daily FX/yields are the working numerical coverage. Intraday equities, indices, sectors, capital, FII/DII, commodity and crypto entitlements are absent.
- No broker execution, subscriptions, activated pricing or purchases. Proposed Advanced credit economics remain a concept; current execution has explicit free anti-abuse bounds.
- Manual transactions must be chronological and in one base currency. Tax lots, corporate actions, benchmark returns, full CSV transaction mapping and portfolio valuation are not complete. Existing legacy CSV/holdings tools remain in the archive.
- Browser screenshots and local test identities do not certify production multi-person collaboration, external onboarding, low-memory Android devices, WCAG conformance or p75 web vitals.
- Owned records are server-side; no sensitive data is stored in browser local storage. Full-text dataset snapshots are private and never attached to group publication.
